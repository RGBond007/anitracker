# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in AniTracker welcoming and harassment-free for
everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, appearance, ability, or personal
characteristics.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and patient.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Welcome questions and differing levels of experience.
- Accept feedback gracefully and correct mistakes openly.
- Respect privacy and avoid publishing another person's private information.
- Put the project's and community's interests ahead of personal conflict.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or sustained bad-faith argument
- Publishing private information without permission
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional community

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, and other participation that
violates this code. Responses may include a private warning, temporary restriction, or permanent
removal from the community, depending on severity and pattern of behavior.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner using the contact method listed on their
GitHub profile. Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible. Maintainers who
are the subject of a report must not participate in deciding its outcome; if no uninvolved
maintainer is available, an appropriate neutral third party should be asked to review it.

This code applies in project spaces and when someone officially represents AniTracker elsewhere.
